Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students in India

Online safety is very important for students who frequently use social media, online payments, and digital platforms. The following checklist provides simple and practical steps to stay safe online.

#Use Strong and Unique Passwords

Create strong passwords using a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. Avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.

# Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Activate 2FA for important accounts such as email, social media, and banking apps to add an extra layer of security.

# Be Careful with Unknown Links

Do not click on suspicious links sent through emails, WhatsApp, or social media messages. These may lead to phishing websites designed to steal personal information.

 Protect Your Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive details such as your address, phone number, ID proofs, or college information publicly on social media.

# Secure Your UPI and Payment Apps

Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone. Legitimate banks or payment apps will never ask for this information.

# Verify Payment Requests

Before accepting any UPI collect request, verify the sender. Scammers often send fake payment requests pretending to send money.

# Use Trusted Wi-Fi Networks

Avoid logging into banking or payment apps while using public Wi-Fi networks in cafes or public places.

# Keep Devices and Apps Updated

Regularly update your phone, laptop, and apps to protect against security vulnerabilities and malware.

# Report Cybercrime Immediately

If you experience or witness cybercrime, report it through the National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
 or call the Cybercrime Helpline: 1930 for immediate assistance.
